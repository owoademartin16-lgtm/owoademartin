Selected Projects

NYC Taxi Fare Prediction

Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · LightGBM · Streamlit

Estimates what a New York taxi trip should cost before the ride starts, using distance, pickup and drop-off area, time of day, and passenger details. Delivered as an interactive app that returns an instant fare estimate.

Typical error $2.61 · captures about 95% of normal fare variation

Cannabis Gross Sales Prediction

Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Time Series · Streamlit

Forecasts daily gross sales so a retailer can plan stock, staffing, and cash flow. Combines recent sales history, day-of-week and holiday effects, and short-term trends. A simple, transparent model matched more complex ones, which makes it easier for a business to trust and maintain.

Typical daily error $4,444 · captures about 90% of sales movement

Electricity Load Forecasting

Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Random Forest · Time Series · Streamlit

Predicts how much electricity a region will need, hour by hour, using past demand, time of day, weekday versus weekend, and seasonal patterns. Gives grid planners an early view of peak demand so they can avoid over-buying power or running short.

Typical error 1,774 MW · captures about 90% of demand behaviour

Amsterdam Airbnb Nightly Price Prediction

Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · CatBoost

Estimates a fair nightly price for an Amsterdam listing using size, location, review history, and host details. Helps hosts price competitively and helps guests judge whether a listing is good value.

Typical error €83 · accounts for about 70% of price variation

Hospital Patient Experience (HCAHPS) Analysis

Python · Pandas · Seaborn · Healthcare Analytics

Tracks nine years of US hospital patient surveys across ten measures of care. Patient experience peaked in 2019, slipped in 2021, and Care Transition (how well hospitals prepare patients to go home) remained the weakest area. The write-up shows hospital leaders exactly which areas need attention.

2019 peak 72.2% · 2021 average 69.4% · Care Transition 51.0%

Bank Customer Churn Prediction

Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Random Forest · Streamlit

Predicts which of 10,000 bank customers are likely to leave, using account activity, product use, and demographics. About 84% of its yes/no predictions were correct, and it flagged roughly two-thirds of customers who actually left. The results feed a customer segmentation and retention plan.

84% prediction accuracy · 67% of leavers flagged in advance

Retail Performance Analysis & Sales Strategy

Python · Pandas · CatBoost · Retail Analytics · Seasonality

Analyses 125,751 transactions to show what sold, when, and where the money came from. Covers category mix and seasonal demand, and adds a sales forecast plus inventory recommendations for peak periods.

$10.48M net revenue · 125,751 transactions · $1.79M December peak

Credit Risk Analysis and Loan Default Prediction

Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Random Forest · Machine Learning · Streamlit

Studies 1.35 million LendingClub loans to understand why borrowers default and to flag risky loans earlier. Roughly 1 in 5 loans defaulted. The model catches about 60% of defaults, making it a useful early-warning filter rather than a replacement for human review.

19.98% default rate · 60.4% of defaults identified

Fraud Detection & Risk Mitigation in Nigerian Banking Transactions

Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · EDA · Fraud Analytics

Examines 5 million Nigerian retail banking transactions to find when and where card and transfer fraud happens. Nearly all fraud occurred at night, which points to tighter after-hours monitoring and closer checks in high-risk states and channels.

₦2.66B fraud value · 0.80% fraud rate · 99.1% at night

Iowa Liquor Sales Analysis

PostgreSQL · SQL · Window Functions · Data Validation

Uses SQL to analyse 2.92M liquor sales records and answer core business questions on monthly trends, categories, and store rankings. A duplicate-record investigation found 429,996 repeated rows tied to a $76.75M gap between raw and cleaned revenue totals.

$501.56M recorded revenue · 429,996 repeated rows · $76.75M revenue gap

London Fire Brigade Incident Analysis

PostgreSQL · SQL · Python · Data Validation

SQL case study over 358,338 emergency incidents covering category mix, borough rankings, hourly and weekday demand, and year-over-year trends. Most callouts were false alarms or non-fire special services, and demand peaked at 18:00.

86.45% false alarms and special services · 18:00 peak · 2026 ahead year over year from January to July

Nigerian Delivery Operations Analysis

PostgreSQL · SQL · Python · Data Validation

SQL case study over 150,000 deliveries comparing couriers, route durations, and city failure rates. The best and worst couriers were separated by less than one percentage point, while the weakest route segment failed 9.60% of the time.

89.96% delivered · 0.65 point courier gap · 9.60% worst segment
