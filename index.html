01 · about

Analyst first, model second.

I moved into data analytics because I kept running into the same problem: plenty of data, not enough clear answers. Now I spend my time cleaning datasets, exploring them properly, and building models that hold up on unseen data.

I studied Computer Science at the Federal University of Petroleum Resources (FUPRE), specializing in Data Science, which gave me the foundation for this work: programming, statistics, databases, and machine learning, applied to real datasets from day one.

My day-to-day stack is Python with Pandas and Scikit-learn, plus SQL, Power BI and Excel when the job calls for it. I like work I can measure. A lower error metric, a forecast that tracks reality, a dashboard someone actually opens.

Most of my projects end up as interactive Streamlit apps, so the model isn't trapped in a notebook: anyone can try it with their own inputs.
